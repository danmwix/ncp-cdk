About NCP-CDK Kenya

A movement shaped by lived experience, guided by purpose, and strengthened through collective voice.

NCP-CDK — the Nexus of Caregivers and Parents of Children with Disabilities Kenya — is a united platform where families driving change come together. When caregivers and parents stand together, they do not only respond to challenges — they redefine what is possible.

Built step by step through advocacy, engagement, and continuous effort, NCP-CDK works to ensure that dignity, rights, and inclusion are not just ideas — but lived realities for every child with a disability and their family across Kenya.

Our vision is clear: a future where every child with a disability is fully included, supported, and empowered — from isolation to inclusion, from silence to voice, from exclusion to recognition.
