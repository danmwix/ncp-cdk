🏛️ Government Support Programs

📋 NCPWD Registration

National Council for Persons with Disabilities registration ensures your child has official documentation for disability benefits and support services.

✓ Access to special needs education allowances
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Inua Jamii Programme

Social protection program providing cash transfers to vulnerable households, including those with members with severe disabilities.

✓ Monthly stipend support for qualifying families
🏥 NHIF Coverage

National Health Insurance Fund covers medical services including rehabilitation, therapies, and hospital visits.

✓ Healthcare coverage for registered members
